Rhys Inward

I’m a final-year DPhil candidate at the University of Oxford and a field epidemiology fellow with the UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA) in Liverpool. I use pathogen genomes, case data, and climate and mobility data to understand how dengue, chikungunya and other infections spread.
